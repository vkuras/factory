# Product context template

`docs/product.md` holds what every feature of the product builds on. It is created when an MVP is groomed and extended whenever a feature adds users, terms or boundaries. Keep it short and at product altitude. It exists so the stakeholder never has to explain the basics twice, and so the architect agent understands the product as a whole.

```markdown
# <Product name>

## Vision
One or two sentences: what this product is and why it exists.

## Problem
The problem it solves, for whom, and how they cope today.

## Target users
Each user group or role: who they are, what they want, their context of use (e.g. "at the stove with dirty hands, on a phone").

## Core value
What the product must do well to be worth using. Everything else is secondary.

## Non-goals
What the product is deliberately not, so no feature drifts there.

## Glossary
Domain terms with their exact meaning in this product. Features reuse these terms instead of inventing synonyms.

## Requirements index
| # | Title | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | ... | MVP | Groomed |
```
