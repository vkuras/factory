# Rezepte

## Vision
A private recipe book for one household, usable at the stove.

## Problem
Recipes live as Markdown files in a Git repo. Only the developer in the household can use them; nobody else does.

## Target users
- Household member: 2–4 people living together. Browses, cooks from and adds recipes, mostly on a phone or tablet in the kitchen. Shops from the shared shopping list on a phone in the store, often without reception.

## Core value
Find a recipe fast and cook from it comfortably, together as a household, and shop for it from one shared shopping list.

## Non-goals
- No public sharing, no social features, no ratings or comments.
- No nutrition information.
- No pantry or inventory management.

## Glossary
- Household: the group of people sharing one recipe collection.
- Recipe: title, category, base servings, ingredients (quantity, unit, name), steps, optional photo.
- Category: Hauptgerichte, Beilagen, Soßen.
- Servings: number of portions; changing it rescales ingredient quantities.
- Cooking mode: step-by-step view with large text; the screen stays on.
- Shopping list: the one shared list of things to buy per household (see 002).
- Entry: one line on the shopping list (name, optional quantity, department, origins, open or checked).
- Own entry: a shopping list entry added by hand, not from a recipe.
- Department: store area used to group the shopping list (fixed list, see 002 R8).
